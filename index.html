About the studio

One artist, many mediums — and a stubborn love of craft.

Quam Art & Design is a one-person studio. That means the person sketching your logo is the same person painting the mural, coding the website and stitching the sample tee. Nothing gets lost in a hand-off.

I move between paint and pixels on purpose. A brand built by someone who also illustrates and paints simply looks more alive — more like a person made it and less like a template found it. That's the whole point.

Currently taking on a small number of brand, illustration and clothing projects. If it needs a real point of view, we'll get on well.
