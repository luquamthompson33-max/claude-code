## 1. Create Your Utopia — "I have a vision, help me make it real"

**Who they are:** Creators, early entrepreneurs, and side-hustlers with a dream in their
head — a brand, a product, a business — who want to turn it into something real, creatively
or with AI. They're capable and ambitious but not designers, not developers, and not sure
what to do first.

**Main problem I solve:** Turn their vision/passion into a real, working thing — brand identity,
apparel, websites, and AI systems that do the busywork for them.

**What I offer:** Design (logos, websites, shirt designs, branding), apparel printing (reasonably
priced), and AI consulting + automations. Pricing still being finalized; apparel already priced fair.

### Pain points (what's stopping them)
- _"great business idea and don't know where to start and are overwhelmed by all of the
  'business' or 'branding' coaches"_ — verbatim, small-business branding discussion
  ([idsacademy](https://idsacademy.gumroad.com/l/BqYeT))
- _"Most small business owners create their first logo themselves or pay someone $20 on a
  freelance marketplace, and it shows."_ — verbatim ([Cowbird Creative](https://www.cowbirdcreative.com/blog/true-cost-of-cheap-logo-design))
- _"Many small business logos look like they were created in Powerpoint or a Word Document."_
  — verbatim, branding fails roundup ([Tailor Brands](https://www.tailorbrands.com/blog/business-logo-fails))
- On AI: _"wasting hours on repetitive manual tasks,"_ _"data scattered across tools,"_
  _"struggling to respond to clients quickly,"_ _"spending too much time on administrative work"_
  — verbatim pain list ([AI Tools Decoder](https://aitoolsdecoder.substack.com/p/the-beginners-guide-to-using-ai-in))
- _"I don't even know what to automate"_ / too many tools, tight budget, afraid of picking wrong _(theme, solopreneur AI threads)_

### Fears
- Looking cheap / amateur and losing credibility before they even start — _"cheap branding
  charges interest, costing you more over time than you realize"_ — verbatim ([Cowbird Creative](https://www.cowbirdcreative.com/blog/true-cost-of-cheap-logo-design))
- Wasting money on the wrong tool or "collecting subscriptions" that never pay off _(theme)_
- Their idea staying stuck in their head forever

### Dreams / goals
- Ship a real brand people take seriously — _"Most people won't buy from you if they don't
  know the meaning behind your brand"_ — verbatim, brand-story advice ([search synthesis](https://www.aol.com))
- Get their time back — automation that gives them _"back 10–40% of their workday"_ and
  saves _"5–10 hours per week on admin tasks"_ — verbatim ([get-alfred.ai](https://get-alfred.ai/blog/best-ai-tools-for-solopreneurs), [entrepreneur.com](https://www.entrepreneur.com/business-news/7-ai-tools-solopreneurs-are-using-to-build-7-figure-businesses-with-no-team-and-no-stress))
- Run lean — _"automate up to 80% of repetitive tasks without hiring a developer"_ — verbatim ([AI Toolkit](https://aiempirestore.gumroad.com/l/AIToolkitforSmallBusiness))

### Exact phrases that repeat (steal these for copy)
- "I have the idea, I just don't know where to start"
- "I'm not a designer" / "it looks unprofessional"
- "I can't afford an agency"
- "so many tools, which one do I even use?"
- "I want to automate this but I don't know how"
- "start with one automation" / "one tool per job" _(the advice they're already being given — meet them there)_

### How I talk to them (angle)
Position CYU as **the person who turns your vision into a real, working brand — and then
automates the parts you hate.** Not a coach, not theory. Show the before/after: their idea →
a shipped logo/site/shirt/automation. Lead with proof, not promises.

**Next action today:** Post one "vision → reality" transformation on IG/YouTube — a real piece
of design or a simple automation you built — captioned with their exact phrase: *"You have the
vision. You're just not a designer / don't know where to start. That's the whole job."*

## 2. Dumb Jocks — "Fuck labels, I define myself"

**Who they are:** Athletes, sports enthusiasts, streetwear and culture lovers, and creatives —
people who refuse to let a label define them. Especially athletes coming up in the inner city
whose stories don't get told. Half-zombie art style, Dennis Rodman-inspired energy.

**Main problem I solve:** Give athlete + street culture a brand that represents *them* — where
people define themselves through what they wear, and inner-city athletes get repped and their
stories told (NIL included).

**What I offer:** Branded clothing ($20–$150 depending on the item) and — the bigger play —
NIL deals for athletes.

### Pain points / what they feel
- Being boxed in by labels — the whole reason streetwear exists: _"a rebellion against monotony,
  a celebration of individuality"_ — verbatim ([Flossy](https://flossy.ee/blogs/news/street-fashion-the-pulse-of-youth-culture-and-self-expression))
- Wanting to be *seen* accurately — fashion as _"a powerful language of self-expression and
  identity construction... to communicate complex personal narratives, social positions, and
  cultural affiliations"_ — verbatim ([The Lord Drip](https://www.thelorddrip.com/blogs/news/how-streetwear-became-the-voice-of-youth-culture))
- Inner-city athletes: talent without a pathway or platform _(theme, youth-sports orgs)_

### Fears / what they push against
- Blending in / looking like everyone else — they want _"attainable... regular, everyday people
  going about their regular, everyday lives,"_ not fake aspiration — verbatim, on r/Streetwear's
  appeal ([AOL/synthesis](https://www.aol.com/reddit-r-streetwear-forum-internet-121745552.html))
- Being defined by where they're from instead of who they are

### Dreams / goals
- Say something without saying it — _"Clothes can say more than words ever could"_ — verbatim ([search synthesis](https://smokedtimes.com/blogs/t-shirts/role-fashion-youth-culture))
- Feel powerful in what they wear — Billie Eilish: _"Oversized clothes make me feel powerful.
  It's about expression, not exposure."_ — verbatim ([BLUORNG](https://bluorng.com/blogs/news/fashion-quotes-for-instagram))
- For the athletes: get put on — NIL orgs sell exactly this: _"NIL opportunities, athlete
  development, grassroots basketball, exposure camps, and strategic mentorship... on and off
  the court"_ — verbatim ([National NIL Sports](https://www.nationalnilsports.com/))
- Build character + a future through the game — _"develop strong, confident student-athletes
  who lead with integrity and vision"_ — verbatim ([Inner City Youth Foundation](https://icyfmn.org/))

### Exact phrases / values that repeat
- "self-expression" / "individuality" / "tell your own story"
- "don't fit in a box" / "define myself"
- "attainable, not aspirational" (regular people, real life)
- "the meaning behind the brand" — _"Most people won't buy from you if they don't know the
  meaning behind your brand"_ — verbatim ([search synthesis](https://www.aol.com))
- NIL: "exposure," "development," "on and off the court," "grassroots"

### How I talk to them (angle)
Lead with the moto — **"Fuck Labels, I Define Myself"** — as the brand's spine. Every drop and
every post is a story: the athlete, the come-up, the identity. Make the inner-city athlete the
hero, not a mascot. NIL is the trust-builder: you don't just sell them a shirt, you put them on.

**Next action today:** Post one athlete's story (yours or a local player's) in the half-zombie/
Rodman visual style with the caption *"They gave you a label. Wear the one you chose."* — and
name-drop that Dumb Jocks is building NIL for real players, DMs open.

## 3. WAHONDAGUY — "Just help me get a car without the games"

**Who they are:** People in the market for a car — many nervous, many first-timers — who dread
the dealership dance. Local to the Auburn / Tacoma / South Sound area.

**Main problem I solve:** Help them find the right car. Free to them — they just pay for the car.

**What I offer:** My consulting/guidance through the whole buying process.

### Pain points / fears
- The whole thing feels overwhelming and stressful _(theme, first-time-buyer threads,
  [AAA](https://magazine.northeast.aaa.com/daily/life/cars-trucks/merging-into-life-podcast-car-buying-tips/))_
- Feeling stonewalled — buyers describe dealers who are _"difficult to work with, not willing
  to give any data, and not willing to negotiate"_ — verbatim, buyer report ([search synthesis](https://www.teamblind.com))
- Getting a bad deal — _"not getting a good offer from 2 dealerships"_ — verbatim ([search synthesis](https://www.teamblind.com))
- The "salesman speel" — the pressure and the pitch they can feel coming

### What they wish for (this is my whole pitch)
- No pressure, someone who makes it easy — reviewers rave about salespeople who are _"super chill,
  no pressure"_ and make it _"kind of fun,"_ who _"put my mind at ease,"_ _"no usual salesman speel,"_
  and _"go above and beyond to make the experience as easy as possible"_ — verbatim review language
  ([Trustpilot/AAA synthesis](https://www.trustpilot.com/review/carlingo.co.uk))
- Honesty — _"no high pressure sales tactics, very honest and professional"_ — verbatim ([Trustpilot synthesis](https://www.trustpilot.com/review/carlingo.co.uk))
- The feeling they're chasing: _"best experience I have ever had buying a car"_ — verbatim ([review synthesis](https://info.oregon.aaa.com/dt_testimonials_category/auto-buying/page/2/))

### Buyer advice they're already repeating to each other (know it, so you're the guy who already does it)
- _"Be ready to walk out if you don't like the dealership no matter how much you want the car"_ — verbatim
- _"Negotiate on car price, not payment price, then go into interest and payments afterward"_ — verbatim
  (both from first-time-buyer advice, [search synthesis](https://www.teamblind.com/post/advice-for-the-first-time-car-buyer-wknhoiml))

### Exact phrases that repeat
- "no pressure" / "made it easy" / "put my mind at ease"
- "honest" / "not pushy" / "no salesman speel"
- "best experience I've ever had buying a car"
- "overwhelming" / "nervous" / "first car"

### How I talk to them (angle)
Be the anti-dealership guy inside the dealership. **"No pressure. No games. I'll just help you
find the right car."** Every WAHONDAGUY post = you removing one fear: transparent pricing,
walking them through it, being the honest local face. You're free — that alone breaks the frame.

**Next action today:** Post one 30–60 sec video answering a real first-time-buyer fear (e.g.
*"Negotiate the car price, not the monthly payment — here's why"*), sign off with *"No pressure,
no speel — I'm just the guy who helps you get the right car. DM me."*

@WAHONDAGUY / Create Your Utopia / Dumb Jocks signature: honest, story-driven, done-for-you.
