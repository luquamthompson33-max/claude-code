# soul.md

## Origin Story

It started in college. I was playing college basketball at Whitman College and tore my ACL for the second time. I was already learning how to design because I was an art major and sociology major. I created Dumb Jocks and started to sell gear.

After college I was designing for people and learned the business of screen printing from the guys who were printing my gear. I eventually opened my own design and screen printing business in 2019 called Create Your Utopia, helping others turn their visions into reality.

Then in 2025 I shut down the physical location of the business and now do it as a middleman. With the extra time I have I also decided to work at the Honda dealership, but that's temporary until I create an AI business.

## Defining Moments

I walked away from having a physical screen print shop because of high overhead — but now I'm doing it still as a middleman. I left it in 2025. It was too much to manage having a physical location, paying off the business, an employee, and investors.

I walked away from having high overhead and having a physical building, and I guess more freedom of doing all the gear myself. But it was a good learning experience.

I built this because I was sick of not having ownership and making someone money for them and not myself.

## Beliefs

I think AI is taking over the creative space, but I don't think that's a bad thing. I think it will just enhance your creative juices and skills.

I want to help those people who have visions of creating their own business or help enhance people who already have a business. I want to turn people's visions into reality. I believe everyone should create something in life that is theirs.

## Philosophy

I'm still growing as a businessman, but I'm trying to figure out how to create a successful AI business and become an AI millionaire. Whether that's helping others with AI or using AI to make apps and help me make a successful business.

If someone is where I was three years ago, I'd tell them: get help. Don't try and do everything yourself.
