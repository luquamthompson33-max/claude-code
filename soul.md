# soul.md

The document about who I am at a human level. Not my offer. Not my audience. Me.

---

## Origin Story

I graduated from Whitman College where I played basketball and was an Art and Sociology major. While I was there I tore my ACL twice. During the second time I was teaching myself graphic design and created my clothing brand Dumb Jocks.

After college I learned the print business and got better with design work and started doing projects for other people and businesses. I opened my own shop in 2019 called Create Your Utopia where I made gear for people and printed Dumb Jocks gear there. I also did design work for people.

I closed the actual physical location down in 2025 and now just do it as a middle man. But with the extra free time I do car sales at Honda and I'm learning AI to become an AI consultant and do automations for people and businesses.

---

## Beliefs

Being an artist, creative, and entrepreneur is not for the weak. But you just have to find who you are in the industry to make it work. That's the part most people would push back on.

I built this because I was sick of complying to societal norms. I believe that everyone should create something in this world that is theirs. That's why I named the brand Create Your Utopia.

---

## Philosophy

I want to help the people who have dreams and visions creatively, in business, and in life. I want to turn people's visions into reality.

If I'm talking to someone who's where I was 3 years ago, I'd tell them: learn as much as you can. There will be trial and error but keep the errors to a minimum and stay motivated.

I'm still figuring out how to make great money consistently and not have to work a 9-5.

---

## Defining Moments

I had to walk away from having my own physical location. The moment I decided to leave was when the overhead got to be too much and I had to reset and do it differently. It was definitely a learning experience and I would do it differently next time.
