06 — Rules of the road

Principles

01

Black is the ground

Near-black is the default. Light surfaces are the exception, used for punctuation, not comfort.

02

One acid, held back

A single lime does all the accent work. Restraint is what makes it hit — if everything glows, nothing does.

03

Type is the image

Oversized grotesk and ghosted background words carry the layout. Photos support; letters lead.

04

Leave a human mark

Marker script, hand arrows, and off-angle rotation keep it raw. Perfect grids get one deliberate break.

Do
  • Set headlines huge, tight, and uppercase
  • Use [ bracketed mono ] for every small label
  • Reserve marker script for 1–2 hero words
  • Keep photos grayscale + high contrast
  • Let one ghost word bleed off the canvas
Don't
  • Introduce a second bright accent color
  • Use lime for long runs of body text
  • Round corners or add soft drop shadows
  • Center-align paragraphs or go timid on scale
  • Let it read templated, safe, or AI-generic

some stories are better written in ink than in words
