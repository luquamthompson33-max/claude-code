01 — Brand Foundation

A running community
with the ambition of flight

RUNISM is a movement, not just a run club. The brand borrows the discipline and optimism of aerospace — precision marks, deep space, the thin blue line of atmosphere — and points it at the most human act there is: putting one foot in front of the other, again.

Essence

Motion, altitude, community. Every runner is a launch.

Personality

Precise, calm, quietly bold. Confident enough to whisper.

Promise

Run again. There is always another horizon and a crew waiting on it.

02 — Logo

The Spark & the Wordmark

The mark is a four-point spark — a compass star stretched along the horizon line. It reads as a burst of speed, a distant star, and a runner's stride at full extension. The wordmark is set in a clean grotesque with generous 0.42em tracking. Together they form the primary lockup.

04 — Typography

One grotesque, three jobs

The system runs on a single grotesque family across three roles: a heavy expanded cut for display, a medium tracked cut for the wordmark and labels, and a neutral cut for body. Headlines are always uppercase; body is always sentence case.

08 — Voice & Tone

Say less. Mean more.

RUNISM speaks in short, certain lines. Confident but never loud. Communal, never corporate. Optimistic about distance and about people.

Do

“We are Runism. Run again.” — short, communal, forward. Use “we” and “community”. Let white space carry weight.

Don't

Avoid hype, jargon, and exclamation stacks. No “crush your goals!!!”. No fear-based fitness talk.

Tagline

Run again.

Rally

We are Runism. Run again community.

Ethos

New generation. Endless horizon. Every runner is a launch.
